Legal Affairs Registry

The Legal Affairs Registry is the component registry of the Public Information Registry (PIR) maintained by the Office of Legal Affairs of the International Organization for Identity Documents. It is the authoritative source for all legal instruments, treaty status records, and related documentation within its competence.

The Legal Affairs Registry forms part of the federated Public Information Registry (PIR) established under the Regulation on the Adoption, Promulgation, and Registration of Instruments of the International Organization for Identity Documents. The General Secretariat maintains a central index aggregating all component registries of the PIR.

About the Legal Affairs Registry

The Legal Affairs Registry is maintained by the Office of Legal Affairs on behalf of the General Secretariat. It is the authoritative source for all instruments within its competence. In the event of any discrepancy between entries in the central index of the PIR and entries in this component registry, the entries in this registry shall prevail for instruments within its competence.

All instruments registered here are publicly accessible. Requests for certified copies of instruments or treaty status certificates should be addressed to the Office of Legal Affairs.

The Legal Affairs Registry is a component registry of the Public Information Registry of the International Organization for Identity Documents, established under the Regulation on the Adoption, Promulgation, and Registration of Instruments.
