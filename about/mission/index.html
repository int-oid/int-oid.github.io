Mission & Objectives

The International Organization for Identity Documents (IOID) is dedicated to advancing the security, interoperability, and universal recognition of identity documents worldwide.

Our Mission

To establish and maintain global standards that enable secure and seamless cross-border mobility, foster digital trust, and protect the fundamental right to identity.

Strategic Priorities

1. Global Standardization

Developing robust technical specifications for passports, national ID cards, and digital identity credentials to ensure global interoperability.

2. Security & Fraud Prevention

Combating identity fraud through advanced security features, secure issuance processes, and international cooperation.

3. Capacity Building

Assisting member states in implementing IOID standards through technical assistance, training programs, and knowledge sharing.

4. Digital Transformation

Leading the transition towards secure digital identity ecosystems, including mobile driving licenses (mDL) and digital travel credentials (DTC).

5. Inclusivity & Human Rights

Ensuring that identity systems are accessible, inclusive, and respect privacy and data protection principles.
